# Social Science Toybox research provenance

## Sources and comparison group

The toybox uses the 16 November 2026 revision of Park et al., including the Methods, SI §4, §9.5 and §9.13. The comparison group is Park’s wave-1 human sample of 1,052 U.S. adults and the study’s Claude Opus 5.5 survey-plus-interview run, `final_opus55_composite`. The model predicted held-out answers. The historical papers below establish the earlier paradigms. Each numeric comparison uses the human and AI data from Park’s study, with the original instrument’s units and condition. [Paper and supplement](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.10109v4#page=34) · [Preregistration](https://osf.io/gk45t/) · [Public replication archive](https://osf.io/hfgeb/)

The archive’s `PROVENANCE.txt` identifies build `2026-11-15r`, source commit `fa065751fa5863d67fcba6486d3a03776e371fca`. The downloaded ZIP’s SHA-256 was `be73fce5b9f02c0dda784eb4bc7d178bd590bac9180d402af1fa978b2ff391bf`. Its `DATA_TERMS.md` allows use of public instruments and aggregates with citation. The replication code is MIT-licensed. Per-person human and AI answers require academic access and a Stanford data-use agreement. The [StanfordHCI genagents repository](https://github.com/StanfordHCI/genagents) provides MIT-licensed software, example agents and a demographic agent bank. The November OSF archive supplies the instruments and outcomes for the run used here. This site uses paraphrased instructions and published aggregate facts.

## Reproduce the displayed data

1. Download the [public OSF archive](https://osf.io/download/hfgeb/). Read `DATA_TERMS.md` and use the existing aggregates. Running the study pipeline or accessing restricted files is unnecessary.
2. For question wording and response options, use `data/public/questions.parquet`. The archive’s legacy game CSV also documents the instructions, but the current parquet corrects the range metadata. The economic-analysis item keys in question order are `PA_DG_1`, `send_pub_1`, `tgreturn_1`, `DV_coop` and `Q134`.
3. Read `analysis/out/task_metrics.json → econ_games.opus55_composite.per_item`. Use `mean_human_w1`, `mean_pred` and the paired `n`. The Prisoner’s Dilemma fields are binary Orange shares, converted to percentages. The other means are already in dollars or percentage points. `analysis/task_metrics.py` drops any pair missing an in-range human or AI answer. This excludes two Public Goods pairs where the human entry was out of range. `analysis/out/distortions.json` and SI §9.13 give available-case benchmarks for verification, with a different denominator for the Public Goods AI mean.
4. For the social studies use `analysis/out/camerer_study_level.json → participants_wave1` and `agents.opus55_composite`. For Schilke, divide each `count_a` or `count_b` by its matching `n_a` or `n_b`. A is low and B is high, verified against the Study 38 instrument and its replication report. `analysis/camerer_study_level.py` restricts simulated answers to the condition the represented human saw.

## Web adaptation and release

The toybox removes real stakes and matching. The displayed payoffs follow the rules for hypothetical counterpart choices. Games are independent and replayable. Power and Trust draws one arm at the start of each play with equal probability and reveals both arms only after the response. All choices are held in component state for the current play. There is no response collection, persistence, consent flow or visitor comparison cohort.

The index is `/experiments/social-science-toybox`. Append `/dictator`, `/trust-sender`, `/trust-returner`, `/public-goods`, `/prisoners-dilemma` or `/power-and-trust` for direct essay links. The return link points to Peter’s Substack until the essay’s final permalink is available. Only the draft branch is excluded from automatic Vercel deployments by the setting in `vercel.json`.
